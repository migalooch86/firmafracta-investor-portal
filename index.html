About FirmaFracta

FirmaFracta is a fintech platform pioneering fractional ownership and usage-based dividend distribution for academic research. We enable researchers, publishers, and investors to share in the economic value of high-impact articles through transparent, blockchain-backed revenue sharing. Authors earn ongoing compensation as their work is read, cited, and applied—not just at publication. Publishers unlock new revenue streams without cannibalizing subscriptions. Investors gain exposure to diversified research portfolios with returns tied to real-world usage metrics. Built on smart contracts with full compliance integration (KYC/AML/accreditation), FirmaFracta transforms academic publishing from a zero-sum licensing model into a collaborative ecosystem where all stakeholders benefit from research impact.

  • Blockchain-Backed Revenue Sharing
  • Smart Contract Automation
  • Full KYC/AML/Accreditation Compliance
  • Real-World Usage Metrics
